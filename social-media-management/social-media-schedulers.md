# Social Media Schedulers

Social media schedulers help teams plan, queue, and publish content across supported social networks from a centralized workflow.

## What a Scheduler Does

Typical scheduling software can provide:
- Content calendars
- Publishing queues
- Scheduled posts
- Media management
- Team approval workflows
- Multiple social profiles
- Basic analytics

The exact platform support and publishing capabilities vary by product.

## When to Use One

A scheduler is useful when the main problem is consistency and coordination rather than content creation itself. It can move a workflow from `Create → Remember to post → Manually publish` to `Plan → Prepare → Schedule → Monitor`.

## What to Compare

| Capability | Questions to Ask |
|---|---|
| Platforms | Which networks are supported? |
| Media | Are images, video, links, and carousels supported? |
| Scheduling | Can posts be queued or bulk scheduled? |
| Teams | Are approvals and roles available? |
| Analytics | What performance data is included? |
| Automation | Can content enter the workflow automatically? |
| AI | Does AI assist creation or optimization? |

## Scheduler vs Automation Platform

A scheduler normally focuses on **when content is published**. An automation platform may cover a broader workflow including content discovery, account-level rules, monitoring, publishing, and repetitive operations.
